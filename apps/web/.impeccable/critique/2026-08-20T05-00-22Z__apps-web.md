## Design Specificity Verdict

Partly authored but category-interchangeable. The muted midnight/green palette, semantic money colors, and tabular numerals feel coherent and calmer than banking dashboards. The familiar card grid has no CashTracker-specific guidance or decisive action, and hard-coded English undermines the Spanish-first product promise.

## Overall Impression

The dashboard is calm and legible, but presently behaves more like a report than a daily cash-flow ritual. The largest opportunity is to turn the balance view into a confident, obvious path to record a movement.

## What's Working

- Summary, spending, recurring, and recent transactions are well grouped with restrained borders and tabular numerals.
- Currency context and income/expense color semantics preserve financial trust.
- The semantic desktop table becomes legible mobile rows; touch targets are generally generous.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Spanish-first promise breaks on the dashboard

**Why it matters:** Headings, labels, date/month formatters, statuses, and empty/error copy are English while navigation is localized, which reduces comprehension and makes the product feel unfinished.

**Fix:** Externalize all dashboard strings/statuses to `messages/{es,en}.json`, derive `Intl` locale from the active locale, and use natural Spanish labels such as “Saldo del mes”, “Pendiente”, and “Registrado”.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`.

### [P1] No clear primary action from the dashboard

**Why it matters:** Especially for an empty month, users are told data is absent but are not taken to “Añadir movimiento”; first-time confidence collapses at the activation moment.

**Fix:** Add a persistent mobile-first “Añadir movimiento” action, an empty-state CTA, and “Registrar” for pending recurring items.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable onboard`.

### [P1] Mobile hamburger is a non-control

**Why it matters:** A plain icon reads as interactive but has no behavior while bottom navigation already exists.

**Fix:** Remove it or make it a labeled button that opens a purposeful account/settings menu.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

### [P2] Recurring card creates a reconciliation burden

**Why it matters:** The rule that items are excluded from totals introduces uncertainty without a resolution action.

**Fix:** Show pending impact separately, add “Registrar” / “Ver recurrentes”, and provide a concise Spanish explainer.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

### [P2] Desktop navigation hides labels until hover/focus

**Why it matters:** First-time and keyboard users must memorize five icons.

**Fix:** Keep a compact always-labeled sidebar or a visible section label for the active item.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (power user):** no quick-add, keyboard shortcut, transaction drill-down, or fast reset to the current month.
- **Sam (accessibility):** desktop icon-only navigation and an inert mobile menu icon compromise affordance; language mismatch breaks expectations.
- **Independent professional with cash-flow anxiety:** pending recurrent items require manual reconciliation against the balance and cannot be recorded in place.

## Minor Observations

- Mobile truncates transactions to five without “Ver todos”.
- Status badges do not communicate state strongly.
- Dark route tokens are duplicated rather than shared.
- Explain that currency views are separate to prevent misconstrued totals.

## Questions to Consider

- What should a freelancer do within five seconds of opening an empty month?
- Could “saldo disponible” distinguish recorded cash from scheduled obligations without another mental calculation?
- Should CashTracker be a report, or a quiet daily ritual for logging a movement immediately?
